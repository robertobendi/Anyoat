Section II / 2026 On the practice

On the
practice.

A small, owner-led architectural office in Batangas City. Residential and small-commercial work, designed in person with the people who will use the building.

Section II.A The architect

The principal.

The practice is led by an architect who studied and practiced in Manila before returning to Batangas to work close to the projects and the climate the office understands best. The studio is intentionally small: the same person who sits with you on the site draws the plans and signs them.

Before founding the office, the principal worked on residential and institutional projects across Calabarzon, including school additions, a small chapel, and several family houses for first-time clients. The current studio practice continues that line — homes, small commercial work, and the occasional civic addition.

The office works in English and Tagalog, in person and over Messenger, and replies to inquiries within two working days. Site visits across Batangas, Lipa, Calatagan, Taysan, and surrounding municipalities are routine; visits further afield can be arranged.

"Hangin, liwanag, lilim — bago ang plano."

Air, light, shade — before the plan.

Section II.B How we work

Three steps.

Every project moves through the same three steps, in this order, at the pace the site and the family need.

01

Site.

We walk the site together. We sit in the morning sun and the afternoon shade. We note the wind, the neighbors' walls, the water table, the way you already use the lot. No drawings yet.

02

Plan.

We draw — by hand at first, then in CAD. You review three rounds of plans, then we agree on materials, the structural system, and a build schedule the family can live with.

03

Build.

We are on site weekly through construction and on call for the contractor. We sign the building over on a single date with the family present, and we return for a one-year review after move-in.
