## Candidate A — Slow editorial luxury (tropical Filipino restraint)

**One-line signature**: A single 16:11 photograph of a jalousie-windowed Filipino interior sits centered in the upper 55% of the viewport, then 18rem of pebble-cream silence before a 5.5rem Fraunces serif H1 lands flush-left with a single 1px olive rule beneath it — the page reads as one breath of light, one room, one sentence.

**Palette**:
- `#F2F1EB` — Pebble cream — surface
- `#FAF8F2` — Coconut milk — secondary surface
- `#4A453F` — Burnt amaki — ink
- `#6E6E3C` — Olive khaki — brand mark + sole accent
- `#1F1D1A` — Volcanic ink — body emphasis, headlines on dark sections
- `#C8C1AF` — Limestone — hairline rules, captions, disabled states

**Typography**:
- Display: **Fraunces** at weight 300, optical size 144, no swashes — reads as a high-contrast modern with quiet authority
- Body: **Switzer** at weight 400 — a calm humanist sans with full Tagalog character coverage
- Accent / eyebrow: **Switzer** at weight 500, tracked uppercase 0.18em at 0.78rem — used only for folio labels and the rare section eyebrow (no third face — the restraint family earns its silence)

**Hero composition**: One photograph, full-bleed-left to centered-right, no overlay. Beneath the image: 18rem of breathing room, then a tracked-caps eyebrow "01 — BATANGAS CITY · ARCHITECTURAL SERVICES" in olive, then a single Fraunces H1 ("Houses that breathe with the climate.") at 5.5rem desktop / 3rem mobile, weight 300. The olive monogram sits small (32px) in the top-left of the page chrome, never inside the hero. No buttons in the hero — the CTA is a single underlined "Make an inquiry →" sitting two lines under the headline in 1rem Switzer.

**Decorative kit**:
- Hairline limestone rule — between every section, never under headings except the hero
- Olive monogram chip — top-left of page chrome and as the page favicon, nowhere else
- Tracked-caps folio labels (01 / 02 / 03) — top-left of every section, paired with section name
- Generous margin-set captions — small Switzer 0.85rem in burnt amaki, single line, sitting in the outer gutter beneath project photos
- One olive 1px underline on H1 — the only decorative weight on the page

**Voice angle**: Single-statement editorial copy — two-sentence paragraphs, present tense, occasional Tagalog phrase sitting bare on its own line ("Hangin. Liwanag. Lilim.") with no italics or scare quotes.

**Why this fits**: ANALYSIS.md is explicit that the site's primary job is *showing work* and that decoration-dense aesthetics fight the photography — restraint reserves the entire visual budget for the portfolio plates. The audience (middle-class Filipino homeowners + diaspora) reads quiet confidence as "expensive enough to take seriously," which is exactly the credibility lift the Facebook-only presence is failing to deliver. Olive becomes a rare gold-accent moment instead of a noisy wallpaper, which lets the existing monogram do its job without competition.

## Candidate B — Mid-century architectural print × Filipino tropical modernism (Push Pin × Lindy Roy)

**One-line signature**: An asymmetric 12-column grid where columns 1–7 hold a 2pt olive line-drawing of a butterfly-roofed Batangueño bungalow with terracotta-filled jalousies and a mustard sun disc above it, while columns 8–12 stack a stencilled "FOLIO 01" in calamansi mustard above an italic Recoleta H1 set in burnt umber — the page feels printed in three inks on manila paper, not rendered.

**Palette**:
- `#F0EBDE` — Manila paper — surface (with a subtle 4% paper-grain SVG texture overlay)
- `#1F1D1A` — India ink — primary ink
- `#6E6E3C` — Olive khaki — brand ink (line-work, monogram, section heads)
- `#A8442B` — Terracotta brick — secondary ink (fills, dividers, eyebrow rules)
- `#D9A036` — Calamansi mustard — highlight (folio numbers, list markers, hover state)
- `#2C4A3E` — Bottle green — tertiary ink (caption rules, illustration shading)

**Typography**:
- Display: **Recoleta** at weight 500 italic — a geometric serif with the Push Pin warmth that Paul Rand's late posters carried
- Body: **Sentient** at weight 400 — a contemporary serif that sets Tagalog and English at equal weight without losing its print feel
- Accent / eyebrow: **Khand** at weight 600 condensed all-caps — the stencilled folio numbers, the section labels, the small print under plates

**Hero composition**: Type-and-illustration spread, not a photo hero. Left two-thirds: a hand-drawn flat-line architectural illustration in olive linework — butterfly-roofed bungalow, banaba tree, stilted veranda, jalousie cluster — with terracotta brick fills and a calamansi mustard sun overhead. Right one-third: stencilled "FOLIO 01 / 2026" in Khand mustard at top, then italic Recoleta H1 "Bahay, anyo, hangin." at 4.25rem in india ink, then a 3-line Sentient deck in burnt charcoal. A terracotta 4pt rule terminates the hero across the full grid.

**Decorative kit**:
- Olive 2pt line-illustrations — one per major section (palm fronds, jalousie cluster, butterfly roof, stilts, banaba tree)
- Stencilled folio numbers in Khand mustard — top-left of every section (00 / 01 / 02 / 03)
- Terracotta rule weights — 4pt section dividers, 1pt body dividers, half-pt caption underlines
- Calamansi mustard square markers — list bullets, index numbers in nav, page-of-N indicators
- Bottle-green hairline borders — under every plate caption, around inline figure references
- Manila paper grain — a 4% noise SVG sitting on every cream surface

**Voice angle**: Confident bilingual editorial prose with section-letter conceits ("Section II — Sa Tabi ng Dagat / By the Sea"), captions credited in Khand mono-caps ("PLATE 03 — CASA M., LIPA, 2024"), running heads at the top of long-form pages like a printed monograph.

**Why this fits**: The illustration kit means the site reads as finished even when the project photo count is thin — a major win against ANALYSIS.md's "[verify] phone, address, principal, full project list" gap; the page degrades gracefully because the olive linework carries the visual weight, not the (currently missing) photographs. Olive gets promoted from accent to *primary ink*, which honors the brand's actual identity instead of relegating it to a single hover state. The Filipino tropical-modern vocabulary (butterfly roofs, jalousies, banaba) localizes the firm in a way generic minimalism never will, and that matters for diaspora clients scanning for a *Filipino* architect rather than just any architect.

## Candidate C — Editorial collage × Architectural monograph (032c × Phaidon × El Croquis)

**One-line signature**: A magazine-spread hero — left half is a corner-cropped 2/3-height photograph of a concrete-and-narra interior bordered in 2pt oxblood, right half is an 8rem Zodiak serif H1 with a hanging olive drop-cap "A" that occupies four body-text lines, while a tracked-caps "FOLIO 01 — SUMMER 2026" runs vertically up the rightmost gutter and an oxblood handwritten marginal note is pinned to the right edge like a translator's gloss.

**Palette**:
- `#F4F1E8` — Bone — surface
- `#1A1815` — Carbon — ink
- `#6E6E3C` — Field olive — brand accent, drop caps, callout rules
- `#7C2417` — Oxblood — marginalia ink, plate borders, pull-quote underline
- `#E8D9B8` — Raw silk — block-callout backgrounds, pulled-quote panels
- `#9A8F77` — Driftwood — captions, folio numbers, plate credits
- `#0A0908` — Plate black — image borders on dark plates, end-rule typography

**Typography**:
- Display: **Zodiak** at weight 400 — high-contrast serif with the monograph weight of Tiempos Headline, sets Tagalog cleanly
- Body: **Sentient** at weight 400 — contemporary serif that holds its texture in long-form reading
- Accent / eyebrow: **Cabinet Grotesk** at weight 500 small-caps, tracked 0.12em — folio labels, plate credits, all marginalia heads

**Hero composition**: Magazine spread, two equal columns. Left column: corner-cropped photograph of a Filipino residential interior — a concrete-and-narra living room with afternoon light — bordered in a 2pt oxblood frame, capped above by a driftwood Cabinet Grotesk small-caps line "PLATE 01 — CASA M., LIPA, 2024." Right column: oversized Zodiak H1 "Anyong bahay para sa banayad na panahon." at 5rem, with the opening "A" set as an olive drop-cap at ~10rem occupying four lines of the body deck below it. An oxblood-ink handwritten marginal note hangs at the right edge ("lit. 'form-houses for the gentle weather'") with a hairline oxblood rule connecting it to the body line it annotates. A 3rem driftwood "01" folio runs vertically up the rightmost gutter.

**Decorative kit**:
- Olive drop caps — opening paragraph of every essay, every project case study, every About-section anchor
- Folio numbers (01 / 02 / 03) — set vertically in driftwood Cabinet Grotesk, gutter-right of every spread
- Oxblood marginalia — handwritten-style Tagalog glosses pinned to the outer margin, connected to body by hairline oxblood rules
- Raw-silk block callouts — pull quotes lifted into colored panels, set in Zodiak italic 2.5rem with an olive 1px top-rule
- Plate credits — driftwood Cabinet Grotesk small-caps, single line, sitting *above* every image with an oxblood half-rule beneath them
- Carbon end-rule — "—— 01" terminates every article like a printed monograph signature

**Voice angle**: Monograph essays with bilingual marginalia — primary copy in considered English present tense, Tagalog notes pinned to the margin in oxblood handwriting as if a translator were glossing the printed page, captions credited in mono small-caps ("PLATE 03 — CASA M., LIPA, 2024 — east elevation, 14:20").

**Why this fits**: ANALYSIS.md asks the rebuild to convert prospects by *proving* the firm can do the work — the monograph format frames every single project as already-published, already-significant, regardless of whether the firm has 4 projects or 40 (graceful degradation: even one project becomes "Plate 01" of an ongoing folio). The bilingual marginalia honors the brand name's whole conceit — the firm is the union of two languages, and the layout literally puts Tagalog and English in conversation on the page. For diaspora clients scanning out-of-province, the monograph signal reads as "this is the architect whose finished book you'd buy in a museum gift shop," which is exactly the credibility lift the Facebook page is failing to give.

## Self-check

The three candidates are screenshot-distinct: A is one photograph and one sentence floating in pebble-cream silence with olive used as a single hairline accent; B is a multi-ink printed-page composition with hand-drawn olive linework, mustard stencil folios, and terracotta rules on grained manila paper; C is a dense two-column magazine spread with an oversized serif drop cap in olive, oxblood marginalia connecting Tagalog glosses to English body, and vertical folio numbers in the gutter. A is the single restraint candidate (justified — architecture for upper-middle Filipino + diaspora clients genuinely benefits from quiet authority); B commits to a localized Filipino-tropical-modern print identity that promotes olive to primary ink and absorbs sparse content via illustration; C commits to a published-monograph identity that absorbs sparse content via folio framing and turns the bilingual brand name into the layout's spine. No two of them landed in the same family, and none of them slipped into "warm artisan minimalism."
